Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to rent a bike?+
A valid driving licence and one government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, or voter ID).
Is a security deposit required?+
Yes. A refundable deposit is collected at pickup and returned in full once the bike comes back undamaged.
Can I book by the hour?+
Yes, most bikes can be rented hourly or daily — hourly rates apply automatically for short rentals.
Can someone drop the bike off at my hotel?+
Yes, choose "Hotel pickup/drop" when booking and mention your hotel name in the notes field.
What happens if I return the bike late?+
A 30-minute grace period is allowed. After that, the hourly rate applies for the extra time.
Can I cancel my booking?+
Yes — see our Cancellation Policy for details.
