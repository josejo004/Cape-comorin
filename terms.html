Terms, Privacy & Cancellation Policy

Terms & Conditions

By renting a bike from Cape Comorin, you agree to use it responsibly, follow local traffic laws, and return it in the condition it was provided, normal wear excepted.

  • Renters must be 18 years or older and hold a valid driving licence for the vehicle class rented.
  • Bikes must not be taken outside Tamil Nadu without prior written permission.
  • Any damage beyond normal wear will be assessed against the security deposit.

Privacy Policy

We collect the details you provide when booking — name, contact information and rental dates — solely to manage your reservation and provide support. We do not sell your information to third parties.

  • Contact details are used for booking confirmations and, where relevant, follow-up support.
  • Identity documents shown at pickup are for verification only and are not stored digitally by default.

Cancellation Policy

Bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 6 hours before the pickup time.

  • Cancellations within 6 hours of pickup may be subject to a partial charge.
  • No-shows without prior notice forfeit any advance payment made.
  • To cancel, call or WhatsApp us with your booking ID.
